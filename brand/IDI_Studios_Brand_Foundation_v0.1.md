# IDI Studios Brand Foundation v0.1

## Brand architecture

| Layer | Role | Public identity |
|---|---|---|
| Intelligent Decisions Interactive (IDI) | Parent company and IP-holding structure | Decision software and the broader company mission |
| IDI Studios | Game-development and publishing label | The public-facing studio behind original interactive entertainment |
| Conquest: Ascension | Flagship original IP | Android-first persistent fantasy strategy RPG |

`idistudios.io` should become the game-facing home of IDI Studios. The existing Intelligent Decisions Interactive identity remains the parent-company and software identity.

## Positioning

IDI Studios is a founder-led independent game studio building deep, systems-driven games for players who value strategy, mastery, and meaningful progression.

### Positioning statement

> IDI Studios creates deep, systems-driven games in which preparation matters, progress is earned, and victory makes sense.

### Core promise

Respect the player's intelligence, time, and investment.

The studio should be known for:

- Strategic depth without deliberately obscured outcomes.
- Progress earned through play rather than purchased combat dominance.
- Persistent worlds and systems that reward long-term mastery.
- Transparent development grounded in working products, not empty hype.
- Ambitious design executed through a disciplined, founder-led production model.

## Brand pillars

### 1. Decisions matter

Players should understand why they won, why they lost, and what they can change.

### 2. Mastery is earned

Progression, preparation, and knowledge should create power—not gacha mechanics or paid advantages.

### 3. Depth remains readable

Complex systems should create meaningful choices without becoming needlessly confusing. This inherits the parent company's principle: **Clarity over Complexity.**

### 4. Execution before hype

IDI Studios leads with playable systems, demonstrated progress, and honest scope.

### 5. Worlds worth mastering

The studio is building expandable original worlds with enough mechanical and narrative depth to support long-term player investment.

## Recommended studio tagline

# Games built for players who think.

This is the strongest master-brand line because it expresses the studio's audience, design philosophy, and respect for players without tying IDI Studios exclusively to fantasy or strategy.

Supporting lines for campaigns and secondary placements:

- Depth without deception.
- Preparation matters. Progress is earned.
- Build worlds. Master systems. Earn victory.

**Clarity over Complexity** remains the parent-company line and an internal design principle. It should not be replaced; it should sit above the studio philosophy rather than compete with the studio tagline.

## Visual direction

IDI Studios should inherit IDI's established dark navy and gold system while becoming more cinematic and game-focused.

### Core palette

- IDI Navy: `#07121D`
- Deep Navy: `#03090F`
- Burnished Gold: `#D4AF37`
- Light Gold: `#DFBD4C`
- Bone White: `#F7F8FA`
- Muted Steel: `#AEB8C4`

### Identity principles

- Use a precise geometric **IDI** lettermark with **STUDIOS** set beneath or beside it in widely tracked capitals.
- Exploit the visual symmetry of `I-D-I`, but keep the mark legible before introducing symbolism.
- Keep the studio identity modern and genre-neutral. The gothic ornament belongs to *Conquest: Ascension*, not to every future IDI Studios title.
- Use Manrope as the continuity typeface for body copy, navigation, and supporting information.
- Allow each game to introduce its own display typography, artwork, and ornamental language beneath the consistent studio mark.
- Favor dark fields, restrained gold, disciplined spacing, large cinematic imagery, and confident short copy.
- Avoid generic esports styling, neon cyberpunk motifs, mascot logos, faux AAA language, and excessive medieval ornament in the master studio identity.

## Voice

IDI Studios should sound direct, intelligent, grounded, and confident.

Use:

- Concrete descriptions of systems and player experience.
- Honest statements about current development status and scope.
- Active language centered on player agency.
- Confidence earned from what is already working.

Avoid:

- Empty superlatives such as "revolutionary" or "the next generation of gaming."
- Pretending the founder-led studio is a large production team.
- Monetization language that contradicts the fair-play promise.
- Vague lore copy when a clear gameplay promise would be stronger.
