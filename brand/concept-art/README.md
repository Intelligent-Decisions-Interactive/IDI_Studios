# Conquest: Ascension website concept-art pack

Four coordinated, text-free scenes for the IDI Studios website. The source PNGs in this directory are the editable review masters. Optimized WebP files live under `public/assets/concept-art/`.

| Asset | Intended use | Composition notes |
|---|---|---|
| `hero-worlds-worth-mastering.png` | Homepage hero | Primary detail on the right; dark, quiet copy area on the left. |
| `realm-development.png` | Realm-development section | Keep and city on the center-right; open sky across the upper left. |
| `formation-command.png` | Combat/strategy section | Tactical formations read across the full frame; commander anchors the lower right. |
| `ascension-event.png` | Ascension section or closing feature | Centered supernatural event with dark crop-safe space on both sides. |

## Shared art direction

- 1672 x 941 pixels (16:9)
- Deep navy-black, charcoal, blackened steel, muted bone, burnished gold, and restrained ember light
- Cinematic painterly dark-fantasy realism
- No embedded text, logos, UI, borders, or watermarks
- Designed for responsive banner crops; retain the center 60% when making narrow derivatives

The complete generation prompts are recorded in `PROMPTS.md`.
